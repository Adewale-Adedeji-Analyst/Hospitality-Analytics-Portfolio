Dashboards
1. Hyatt Regency Lagos Ikeja — Revenue & Booking Analysis
Tool: Power BI | Dataset: 5,500 rows | Period: Jan 2023 – Dec 2024
A three-page commercial dashboard built on a simulated pre-opening hotel dataset for the Hyatt Regency Lagos Ikeja.
Pages:
	•	Revenue Overview — Total revenue, average nightly rate, booking trends, channel performance, segment breakdown
	•	Booking Analysis — Lead time, nights stayed, booking status, no-show rate
	•	Sales & Guest Performance — Top corporate accounts, discount impact, revenue by sales rep, cancellation losses
Key Insights:
	•	₦470.77M lost to cancellations — flagged need for deposit policy
	•	Zero-discount bookings are highest volume — brand drives demand, not price
	•	OTA revenue closing the gap on direct — commission erosion risk
	•	Most guests book 30+ days in advance — implications for outreach timing

2. Velora Hotel — Booking Performance
Tool: Power BI | Theme: Dark gold
A single-page dashboard tracking booking patterns, loyalty performance and revenue trends for a fictional luxury hotel.
Key Insights:
	•	28.6% cancellation rate — revenue predictability problem
	•	Non-loyalty members dominate bookings — enrolment opportunity at check-in
	•	Weekday vs weekend demand split visualised for yield management decisions

3. HR Staff Retention Dashboard
Tool: Power BI | Dataset: 300 employees across 5 Lagos hotels
Built directly from original research on hospitality staff retention in Lagos. Three pages covering retention drivers, employee wellbeing, and training and development.
Pages:
	•	Retention — Attrition by hotel, department, salary band, and experience level
	•	Wellbeing — Job satisfaction, welfare scores, overtime rates by department
	•	Development — Promotion rates, training hours, undertraining by department
Key Insights:
	•	Promoted employees retain at 96% vs 51% for non-promoted — strongest lever available
	•	High earners retain at only 29% — pay alone does not retain people
	•	Sales department 29% undertrained — commercial risk, not just an HR problem
	•	F&B has lowest job satisfaction and welfare scores across all departments
