# Hyatt Regency Lagos Ikeja Sales Intelligence Dashboard

## Project Overview

This project is a hospitality sales intelligence dashboard built in Power BI to analyze booking behavior, revenue performance, cancellation trends, and customer booking channels within a hotel environment.

The dashboard was designed to simulate real-world commercial reporting used by hotel sales, reservations, and revenue management teams to support operational and strategic decision-making.

## Business Problem

Hotels generate large volumes of booking and revenue data daily, but without effective visualization and analysis, identifying performance trends and revenue risks becomes difficult.

This dashboard was created to answer key business questions such as:

- Which booking channels generate the most revenue?
- How do cancellations affect revenue performance?
- What customer segments contribute most to bookings?
- Are discounts driving meaningful revenue growth?
- Which trends could impact profitability and occupancy?

## Objectives

- Analyze hotel booking and revenue trends
- Identify revenue exposure from cancellations
- Compare booking channel performance
- Evaluate discount effectiveness
- Build an executive-style interactive dashboard for decision-making

## Tools & Technologies Used

- Power BI
- Power Query
- DAX
- Data Visualization
- Data Cleaning & Transformation

## Key Insights

- Cancellation trends revealed significant potential revenue exposure
- OTA booking channels showed strong growth patterns
- Zero-discount bookings contributed heavily to overall revenue
- Revenue concentration patterns highlighted key commercial opportunities
- Seasonal booking trends impacted occupancy behavior

## Dashboard Features
